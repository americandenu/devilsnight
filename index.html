The Curator’s Provenance
Authorized Lineage and Eyewitness History

This is not an urban legend ride. It is an archival field investigation led by the co-organizer of DC’s official Exorcist Day.

In 2015 we brought Academy Award winning director William Friedkin and Academy Award winning author William Peter Blatty to Washington to dedicate the 36th and Prospect Exorcist Steps. Friedkin later told Jake Tapper on CNN that what we did for him on the Exorcist Steps meant more to him than winning his Oscar.

While Blatty wrote the novel, this ride belongs to Friedkin’s vision and obsessive search for the truth. The curator worked directly on Friedkin’s Vatican documentary The Devil and Father Amorth, shot by Robert Yeoman, the celebrated cinematographer behind every Wes Anderson feature film. Long before co-hosting the film’s premiere at Landmark’s E Street Cinema, the curator and Friedkin spent extensive time together across DC and Georgetown. Through those walks and hours of conversation, they forged a bond centered on Friedkin’s artistic philosophy, his unfiltered storytelling, and his fascination with the supernatural. When Friedkin passed away, the curator organized and hosted the public memorial gathering at the Exorcist Steps to honor his life and towering legacy in the city.

While the film made Georgetown world famous, critical chapters of the real 1949 case unfolded five miles east in Brookland. Tonight on the anniversary of the proclamation, we ride to the boy’s real church where it all happened.
